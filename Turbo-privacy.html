Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Turbo Drift: Extreme Racing.

Effective dateNovember 9, 2026 AppTurbo Drift: Extreme Racing Package namecom.turbo.drift.extremeracing
Privacy at a glance Turbo Drift: Extreme Racing does not require an account, does not display ads, does not include advertising SDKs, and does not collect or transmit personal or sensitive user data. Game progress and settings are stored only on your device.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to Turbo Drift: Extreme Racing (the “Game”), published under the Android package name com.turbo.drift.extremeracing. It describes how the Game handles information when installed and played on an Android device.

2. Information We Collect

The Game does not require account registration and does not collect, transmit, or store personal or sensitive user data on our servers. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, device identifiers, or payment information through the Game.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

The Game may save gameplay information locally on your device, including level progress, unlocked vehicles, in-game currency, sound settings, and other preferences. This information remains on your device and is used only to provide the Game’s offline features.

You can delete locally stored information by clearing the Game’s app data or uninstalling the Game. Because this information is not transmitted to us, we cannot retrieve or restore it.

4. Advertising

The Game does not display advertisements, does not include advertising SDKs, and does not access or use the Android advertising ID.

5. Analytics, Crash Reporting, and Online Services

The Game does not use third-party analytics SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs, account services, cloud saves, online leaderboards, or remote configuration services. The Game is designed to function without sending gameplay or device information to us.

6. Third-Party Sharing

Because the Game does not collect personal data, we do not sell, rent, or share personal data with advertisers, data brokers, analytics providers, or other third parties.

7. Support Communications

If you choose to contact us by email, we will receive your email address, message, and any information you voluntarily include. We use that information only to respond to your request. Support messages are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the request, meet legal obligations, or protect our rights, and are not used for advertising.

8. Children’s Privacy

The Game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The Game has no account system, advertising, chat, social features, or in-game mechanism for submitting personal information. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us through a support email, please contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

9. Data Security and Retention

Game progress and settings remain locally on your device until you clear the Game’s data or uninstall it. Any information you voluntarily send by email is handled with reasonable administrative and technical safeguards and retained only as described in Section 7.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the Game’s features, data practices, or legal requirements change. When we make changes, we will update the effective date shown at the top of this page. If a future version adds advertising, analytics, online services, or other data collection, this policy and the Google Play Data safety disclosure will be updated before that version is released.
