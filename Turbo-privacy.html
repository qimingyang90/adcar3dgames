Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Turbo Drift: Extreme Racing (the “Game”). The Game may display advertisements through third-party advertising and mediation service providers. These providers may process certain device, technical, and advertising-related information as described below.

1. Information You Provide

The Game does not require you to create an account. We do not ask you to provide your name, postal address, phone number, or payment information to play the Game.

If you contact us for support, we may receive your email address and any information you choose to include in your message. We use this information only to respond to your request, maintain support records, and protect against abuse.

2. Information Processed Automatically

When you use the Game, third-party advertising, mediation, measurement, attribution, analytics, security, and anti-fraud service providers may automatically collect or receive information such as:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from the IP address;
  • Advertising identifiers, device identifiers, app identifiers, and consent or privacy-choice signals;

  • Game version, package identifier, SDK version, and general app usage or session information;
  • Advertisement requests, availability, impressions, clicks, views, rewards, conversions, and other interactions with advertisements;
  • Crash reports, diagnostic data, performance information, and fraud or security signals.

The exact information processed may vary depending on your device, region, privacy choices, the advertisement shown, and the providers available at that time.

3. Local Game Data

Game progress, preferences, settings, currency balances, unlocked content, and similar gameplay data may be stored locally on your device. This local data may be deleted if you uninstall the Game, clear its storage, reset your device, or use operating-system tools that remove app data.

4. How Information Is Used

Information processed in connection with the Game may be used to:

  • Display contextual or personalized advertisements, where permitted;
  • Select and deliver advertisements and control how often they are shown;
  • Provide in-game rewards associated with rewarded advertisements;
  • Measure advertisement performance, engagement, attribution, and conversions;
  • Analyze technical performance, diagnose crashes, and improve reliability;
  • Detect invalid traffic, fraud, abuse, and security threats;
  • Comply with legal obligations and honor applicable privacy choices.

5. Advertising and Mediation

The Game uses third-party advertising and mediation technology to request, select, and display advertisements. Mediation allows advertisements to be supplied by different advertising partners. The available partners may vary over time, by region, device, ad format, and availability.

Advertising providers may use the information described above to provide personalized advertising where you have consented or where otherwise permitted by law. If you choose non-personalized advertising, advertisements may still be displayed using contextual information, general location, device information, frequency controls, and fraud-prevention signals.

Information may be processed by advertising networks, mediation platforms, advertisers, ad exchanges, demand-side platforms, measurement and attribution providers, analytics providers, anti-fraud providers, and other service providers involved in delivering and measuring advertisements or operating the Game.

These third parties may act as independent controllers or as service providers and may process information under their own privacy policies. Their practices are governed by their own terms. You may contact us if you would like information about the advertising providers currently used by the Game.

We do not directly sell your personal information to data brokers. However, some advertising activities may be considered a “sale,” “sharing,” or “targeted advertising” under certain privacy laws. Where required, we provide choices to limit those activities.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing of your personal information, or to withdraw consent. You may also have the right to opt out of targeted advertising, sharing, or sale as those terms are defined by applicable law.

You can use any consent or privacy controls presented in the Game, as well as your device settings, to manage advertising personalization or reset or limit the use of your advertising identifier. To submit a privacy request, contact us using the email address below. Because some information is processed directly by third-party providers, we may need to direct you to the relevant provider or request additional details needed to identify the applicable data.

The Game does not require an account and does not include public chat or social networking features. We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information directly to us.

Where we know that child-privacy requirements apply, we seek to use age-appropriate advertising and privacy settings and do not knowingly use children’s personal information for personalized advertising. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review and take appropriate action.
