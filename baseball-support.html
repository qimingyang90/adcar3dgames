Frequently Asked Questions

Does the game require an internet connection?

No. InningStack Baseball Challenge is designed as an offline, single-player game.

Does the game collect personal information?

No. The game does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share your personal information. For more details, please read our Privacy Policy.

What should I try if the game is not working correctly?

  1. Close the game completely and open it again.
  2. Restart your device.
  3. Make sure your device is running a supported version of iOS.
  4. Check the App Store for an available game update.

How can I reset or remove my game progress?

Game progress and settings may be stored locally on your device. Deleting the game generally removes its local data. However, your device's backup and restoration settings may restore that data later. Reinstalling the game can permanently remove progress, so please use this option carefully.

Can deleted game progress be restored?

The game does not provide an online account or developer-operated cloud save service. If local progress is deleted and is not available through a device backup, we cannot restore it.
