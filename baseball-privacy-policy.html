Privacy Policy

InningStack Baseball Challenge

Effective date: November 26, 2026

In short: InningStack Baseball Challenge is an offline, single-player game. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how the developer of InningStack Baseball Challenge ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the game (the "Game").

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information or usage data through the Game. The Game does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or other personal information.

2. Offline Gameplay and Local Data

The Game is designed for offline play. Game settings and progress may be stored locally on your device so that the Game can function and remember your progress. This local data is not transmitted to us. You can remove it by deleting the Game from your device, subject to your device's own backup and restoration settings.

3. Third-Party Services

The Game does not currently integrate third-party analytics, advertising, login, social media, or tracking SDKs. We do not share Game data with third parties.

Your download and use of the Game may be processed by the platform from which you obtained it, such as Apple App Store. That platform may process information under its own privacy policy. We do not control the platform's independent data practices.

4. Tracking and Advertising

The Game does not track you across apps or websites and does not contain third-party advertising.

5. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users. Because the Game does not collect personal information, no personal information from children is knowingly stored or shared by us. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us outside the Game, please contact us so that we can address the request.

6. Data Security and Retention

Because the Game does not transmit personal information to us, we do not maintain a server-side database of users or retain personal data collected through the Game. Locally stored game progress remains under the control of your device and its operating system.

7. Your Choices and Data Deletion

Since we do not collect or retain personal information through the Game, there is no personal information held by us for you to access, correct, or delete. You may delete locally stored game data by uninstalling the Game. If you contact us by email, you may ask us to delete the information contained in your correspondence.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the Game's features or data practices change. Any updated policy will be posted at the same location with a new effective date. If a future version of the Game collects information, we will update this policy and provide any notices or choices required by applicable law before that collection begins.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions or requests concerning this Privacy Policy, contact us at pyromu@hotmail.com.
