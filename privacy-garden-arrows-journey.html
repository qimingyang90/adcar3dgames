Privacy Policy for Garden Arrows Journey

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Garden Arrows Journey (the “Game,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) handles information when you use our mobile game. The Game includes third-party advertising services. Please read this Policy to understand the information that may be collected through those services and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

The Game does not require you to create an account. We do not ask you to provide your name, postal address, or payment account details to play the Game. However, our advertising partners may automatically collect or receive certain information when their software development kits (SDKs) operate within the Game, including:

  • Device and advertising identifiers: such as the Android Advertising ID, where available and permitted, and other device or app identifiers used by advertising SDKs.
  • Approximate location: such as country or region inferred from an IP address; we do not require precise GPS location to play the Game.
  • Advertising and app interactions: such as ad requests, impressions, clicks, rewarded-ad completions, and related interaction events.
  • Device, network, and diagnostic information: such as device model, operating system, app or SDK version, IP address, network status, error reports, and performance or diagnostic data, depending on the SDK and its configuration.

The precise data collected can vary based on your device, location, privacy choices, and the versions and settings of the advertising SDKs.

2. Why Information Is Used

Information may be processed to request and display ads, measure ad performance, administer rewarded advertising, prevent invalid traffic or fraud, maintain the reliability and security of advertising services, troubleshoot technical issues, and meet applicable legal requirements. Where permitted and subject to applicable choices or consent requirements, advertising partners may also use information to personalize ads.

4. Your Privacy Choices

You can manage or reset your device's advertising identifier and available ad privacy preferences through Android settings. Changing advertising preferences may affect personalization but does not necessarily remove all advertisements.

We and our providers use measures intended to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system is completely secure.
