Player support

Let’s get you back on the road.

Quick answers and troubleshooting for Turbo Drift: Extreme Racing.

How do I steer?

Tap the on-screen turn control as your car reaches a corner. Timing matters more than speed.

Why did my car leave the road?

The turn was triggered too early or too late. Use the road edge as your timing cue.

The game will not start

Restart the game, check for updates, restart your device, and make sure free storage is available.

Still need help?

Include your device model, Android version, game version, level number, and a short description.
